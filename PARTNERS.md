LOOMO is building a community of businesses, ERP implementors, ERPNext/Frappe experts, developers, freelancers and IT service partners who want to implement and support LOOMO ERP solutions.

We invite expressions of interest from:
- ERP implementation companies and system integrators
- ERPNext and Frappe specialists

- Organizations interested in a future official LOOMO implementation partner program

Potential collaboration areas include ERP deployment, configuration, migrations, integrations, workflow design, training and ongoing customer support.

LOOMO intends to define an official implementation partner program. Certification, branding rights, referrals, commercial terms and official partner status require LOOMO's separate approval and agreements; interest alone does not confer partner status.
