# LOOMO roadmap

*Planning document only; features, commercial availability and dates may change.*

1. **Open-source LOOMO ERP:** audit the ERPNext-derived fork, preserve GPL-3.0 notices and license obligations, check for sensitive information and publish the community code with installation documentation.
2. **Ecosystem:** welcome clients, experienced ERPNext/Frappe implementors, freelancers, developers and potential official implementation partners.
3. **LOOMO AGENT:** develop the commercial AI-agent offering and its integration with LOOMO ERP; review its technical and GPL boundaries.
4. **Enterprise extensions:** release additional appropriately licensed, separately developed modules and services after architecture and license review.
5. **LOOMO ERP + LOOMO AGENT SaaS:** explore a managed subscription offering with commercial AI capabilities and hosting/support.

**Source status:** LOOMO ERP source is currently with a development partner and has not been reviewed or published in this organization.

More information and inquiries: **https://loomo.biz**.
